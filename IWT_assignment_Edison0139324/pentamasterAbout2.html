Our Background

Pentamaster Corporation Berhad (“Pentamaster”), established in 1991 in Penang, Malaysia, is a global provider of automation manufacturing and technology solutions. With over 32 years of experience, we provide core solutions in Automated Test Equipment, Medical Device Automation Technology, Factory Automation Solutions, and Warehouse Automation. We proudly serve a wide range of industries including semiconductors, automotive, medical, and general manufacturing across the globe from the US to Europe, Asia Pacific and Africa. Beyond our Penang HQ and Batu Kawan facility, we operate globally with offices in the USA, Japan, Germany, Singapore and a plant in China. A third facility in Batu Kawan is underway. We believe in building a sustainable future through our ESG efforts and environmental responsibility ensuring that growth doesn’t come at the planet’s expense. Just like a homing pigeon, no matter how far we expand, we always return to our roots in Penang.
